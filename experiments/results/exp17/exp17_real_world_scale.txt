Latency caveat
These are not a latency benchmark. Mean store size is only
40.4 memories, so every arm is far below a millisecond and
fixed per-call overhead dominates. The two paths also differ in
cache treatment: retrieve_top_k_fast reads a cache warmed once per
conversation, while retrieve_semantic_only rebuilds its own (n, 128)
matrix on every call. Exp4 is the latency measurement; read these
figures only as evidence that the composite path stays sub-millisecond
at this scale.

Why recency_only scores the same at k=5 and k=10
Sessions are contiguous blocks of turns, so the most recent turns
nearly always sit inside one session. The recency window holds
1.026 distinct sessions on average, and
0.2763 of queries have a window drawn
entirely from the target session. Such a query scores 1.0 at both k
and every other query scores 0.0, which is why the two columns agree.
It is a property of the corpus layout, not a scoring error.

Reading of the result
NCM does not beat the semantic-only baseline on P@5 under the
deployable condition where the query state is inferred from the
query text. The composite distance does not help on this task.
